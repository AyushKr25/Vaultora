QUERY: 3.4.3 Accounts where total deposits exceed total withdrawals (correlated subquery)
============================================================
SQL: SELECT ba.account_number, ba.balance,
              (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(t.amount),0) FROM transactions t WHERE t.account_id=ba.id AND t.transaction_type='deposit') AS total_deposits,
              (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(t.amount),0) FROM transactions t WHERE t.account_id=ba.id AND t.transaction_type='withdrawal') AS total_withdrawals
         FROM bank_accounts ba
         WHERE (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(t.amount),0) FROM transactions t WHERE t.account_id=ba.id AND t.transaction_type='deposit')
             > (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(t.amount),0) FROM transactions t WHERE t.account_id=ba.id AND t.transaction_type='withdrawal')
